Leaves out a standard English list of linking words, question words and general verbs (hold, make, use...); weights each word by how rare it is in the approved content; compares words on their first five letters. Score 0 to 1.
- **StubStages.cs, DatabaseSearchStage**: after a full-text hit, AnswerExtract picks the quote as before; it is a direct answer only if its score is at or above the direct-answer match mark and it has the shape the question asks for (a number for "how many", a date or period for "when", a role for "who"; the same rule output compliance uses on AI answers). Otherwise database_search returns not found (data: weak_match; match=...; missing=... or shape_missing=...) and the question goes on to search by meaning. A direct hit logs match= and mark=.
