## Full-text search: a direct answer must cover the question (November 10, Willis)
- Problem found in testing: a full-text hit only needed every word somewhere in a passage, and passages are whole sections, so the words could come from different requirements ("How many members must attend each educational event?" was answered with the four-events line). Direct answers then got no check that they answer the question. Of 36 direct answers from today's runs, 19 did not answer what was asked (Claude's reading; not yet checked by a person).
- **Services/Orchestrator/Stages/DirectMatch.cs** (new, no AI): scores the sentence Isa would quote on the question's meaningful words. Leaves out a standard English list of linking words, question words and general verbs (hold, make, use...); weights each word by how rare it is in the approved content; compares words on their first five letters. Score 0 to 1.
- **StubStages.cs, DatabaseSearchStage**: after a full-text hit, AnswerExtract picks the quote as before; it is a direct answer only if its score is at or above the direct-answer match mark and it has the shape the question asks for (a number for "how many", a date or period for "when", a role for "who"; the same rule output compliance uses on AI answers). Otherwise database_search returns not found (data: weak_match; match=...; missing=... or shape_missing=...) and the question goes on to search by meaning. A direct hit logs match= and mark=. The search's own word list and AND search are unchanged on purpose: a longer list and an OR search were tried and moved questions meant for the AI onto unchecked direct quotes.
