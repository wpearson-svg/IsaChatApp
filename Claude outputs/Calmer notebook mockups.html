A calmer succession notebook

The notebook panel as it looks now (Before, copied from Isa on 23 October 2026 at 1440 × 900) next to a calmer version (After). Nothing the notebook can do is taken away: every action is still one or two taps from where it is now. What changes is how much shows at once. The sample entries are tidied too, so they no longer contradict each other. All people, the chapter and the entries are invented.

The ideas behind every "After":

  • One job per screen. The handoff checklist becomes a one-card summary that opens its own page, the same way an entry does.
  • Say it once. One line under the title says what the notebook is. The privacy rule sits in one quiet line at the bottom, with ⓘ for the full wording.
  • One main button. "Add a note" stays. Rarer actions (print all decisions, print one record, remove) move into a "⋯" menu.
  • Only what tells you something. No "PRIVATE" badge on every row, because every entry is private unless it is shared. Shared entries get a small green "Shared with the board" mark instead.
  • Tools when they're needed. Search and the filters appear once a notebook has more than six entries.

1. Sato Yumiko opens the notebook (outgoing president, in her handoff window)

  • Before: the 13 checklist items fill the first screen, and her notes start a screen and a half further down.
  • After: one card shows her progress, the next item and the time left. Her notes are visible straight away. "Open the checklist" opens the full list as its own page (third picture).

2. Watanabe Kenji opens the notebook (incoming president, day 3)

  • Before: four rows of controls (two buttons, search, three filters, a month heading) sit above four entries, and every row carries two or three badges.
  • After: one button and the entries. Each row is a full title and one quiet line: kind, topic, who wrote it and when. Search and filters return once there are more than six entries.
  • His Today step "Read what your predecessor left you" opens this same view.

3. An entry on its own page

  • Before: a five-line details table with a drop-down, three boxed sections, two buttons, a checkbox, a Remove link and a second privacy line.
  • After: the title in large type, one line saying when, who and the topic, then what was asked and Isa's cited answer. "Share with the board" stays on the page as a switch, because it's a privacy choice and should never be hidden. Print, email, change the topic, the record number and Remove go into "⋯".
  • The sample entry now makes sense: the decision matches the question it answers.

4. Opening at half width

  • Now: with only the chat and the notebook open, each takes a third, and the last third shows the dimmed landing page.
  • After: the notebook opens at half the screen and the chat keeps its third (the same sizes "Enlarge" gives today). Entry titles fit on one line and the entry page has room to breathe.
  • When Today opens too, all three go back to a third each, exactly as now. Enlarge still works the same way.
  • This changes the October 20 rule "With two panels open, each is still a third". It needs your decision.
