## Do not click these

They change the demo's data or leave the demo, and the setup script is the only way back.

- **Any time slot, "Write message" or "See next session"** on the contact step. Booking or sending completes the step.
- **"Mark as done"** on any step.
- **"Add to my calendar"** in the calendar. It downloads a file.
- **"Open Engage"** on a board member's page. It opens a new tab.
- **"Ask Sato Yumiko about this"** in the notebook. Point at it only.

The one thing that is meant to change data is the Save at the very end.
