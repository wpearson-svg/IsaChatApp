1. The help section, from the rail

A round ? "Help" button at the bottom of the rail (above Backstage, for staff). It opens in the right-hand place, like the Library and the Notebook (one of the three at a time). Six short sections, with links to jump to each. "Ask Isa" buttons put an example question into the chat box.

1What Isa can help with

  • Answers from ISACA's guidance. Ask about running your chapter. Isa looks only in ISACA's approved documents for chapter leaders, and every answer lists its sources. Open "Show the steps" on an answer to see how she found it.
  • Your first 90 days. Today shows your steps for where you are in your term. Mark them done as you go.
  • The succession notebook. Notes and decisions that pass from one president to the next.
  • The Library. Read ISACA's documents, or listen to them read aloud.

2What Isa doesn't do

  • Isa doesn't give opinions, rulings or advice of her own, and she doesn't search the internet. She only shares what ISACA's guidance says.
  • For legal questions, local laws, disagreements on your board, money matters, or anything personal, talk to a person.
  • If you raise something sensitive, such as harassment, Isa doesn't answer it herself. She passes it to a person and gives you a reference number.

3Questions to try

When do we send in our yearly paperwork?Ask Isa

Tips: ask one thing at a time, in your own words. If ISACA's guidance doesn't cover it, Isa says so and passes it to a person; she won't guess.

4Saving decisions and notes

  • Under an answer, Save to notebook keeps the question, Isa's answer, its sources and the date. You can add a line about what you decided.
  • In the notebook, + Add a note for contacts, what worked, or what's unfinished.
  • Notes are private to you. Share with the board on a note lets your board members see it.

5Reaching a person

  • Talk to a person, under the question box, any time.
  • When Isa passes a question on, she gives you a reference number, like TKT-0012. Keep it if you follow up with ISACA's Chapter Experience team or ISACA Support.
  • In your first week, Meet your Chapter Experience contact (in Today) lets you book a welcome call or send an introduction.

6What happens to your information

  • Isa's logs don't keep the text of your questions. A question is only kept if you save it to your notebook.
  • Your Chapter Experience contact sees your chapter, role and term dates. Your questions to Isa stay private.
  • Your notebook is private to you unless you share a note with your board.
  • Introductions you write in Isa are saved in Isa; Isa never emails them.
