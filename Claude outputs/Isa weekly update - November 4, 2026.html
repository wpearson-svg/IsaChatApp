This week

Search by meaning Built · connecting

Each approved passage is turned into 768 numbers that capture its meaning, and so is each question. Isa finds the closest passages even when they share no words with the question, so leaders don't have to guess the guidebook's exact wording.

Only approved passages can come back. If none is a good enough match, Isa says she couldn't find the answer in ISACA's documents, rather than guessing.

Checking answers Built · testing

When the AI words an answer, a separate checking model scores each sentence from 0 to 1 for whether its source supports it. Numbers, dates and contact details must match the source, and "must" cannot be softened.

Each answer gets a confidence score. If the wording doesn't pass, Isa quotes ISACA's exact words or passes the question to a person. Both pass marks are adjustable on a dashboard, with no restart.

Clear rules for what Isa will and won't answer Self-test passing

Written rules decide, for each kind of question, what Isa does. A rule can cover every chapter, one region or country, or a single chapter. A local rule can add caution but can never remove a protection that applies more widely.

13 sample rules, each with test questions. Files without an approval record are refused. A tester shows what Isa would do with any question, and which rule decided it. In a real deployment, ISACA would write and approve the rules.

Translation Working

Translation works end to end, in many languages. It uses a translation model that runs on our own computers and covers about 200 languages, and we can switch any of them on.

If the model isn't available, Isa shows the original English and says so, rather than pretending she translated it.

Three ways to reach a person Working

Under every answer, a leader can tell Isa whether she helped; if not, she offers to pass it on. "Talk to a person" is always at the bottom of the chat. When an answer also needs a human view, Isa offers a button to pass it to the Chapter Experience team.

The leader decides what goes with the request, and nothing is sent until they press send. Each hand-off gets a reference number and can open a case in Salesforce.

Room to grow Working

Isa's heavier AI services can run on a separate, more powerful computer of our own, over a private, encrypted network. No outside service is involved.

An administrator chooses on the dashboard where the services run and can switch voice and translation on or off. The chat stays light, so Isa grows by adding capacity where it's needed.

Privacy in the logs Built

Every check Isa runs is recorded, so we can always see why she answered the way she did. The records hold counts and categories only, never a leader's words.
