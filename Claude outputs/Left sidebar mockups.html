New: the rail, with an option to hide it

The four buttons sit at the top of the rail, with a line between Isa's own tools and the two ISACA sites. Today shows a green number for the steps due today. "Hide" at the top tucks the rail away; the menu button that appears in the header (with a green dot while steps are due) brings it back. The header keeps only New conversation and Close. The "Your first 90 days" line stays, without its buttons. "Backstage" at the bottom holds Config and the two dashboards, and shows for the Developer, Executive and Manager roles only.
