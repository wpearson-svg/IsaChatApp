Library with Listen: mockups

For Matthew's review, 23 October 2026. Nothing in the app has changed yet. The Library lists ISACA's five documents that Isa answers from. Listen reads the text Isa answers from, word for word, using the browser's own read-aloud (nothing installed, nothing sent anywhere). Sample content only; the exact screens may shift a little when built.

1. The Library, opened from the rail

A new Library button sits on the rail under Notebook, in ISACA Purple (the rail's other colours are taken). It opens a panel beside the chat, like the Notebook. The documents are grouped by topic, each with a short description in Isa's words, about how long it takes to listen to, and two buttons: Read and Listen.

2. Listening: the text, with the sentence being read highlighted

Listen (or Read, then the play button) opens the document's text. The player stays at the top while the text scrolls. The sentence being read is highlighted and kept in view; sentences already read turn grey. Buttons: back a section, play/pause, forward a section, and reading speed. Clicking any sentence starts reading from there.
