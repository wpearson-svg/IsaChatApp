"Meet your board": mockups

For Matthew's review, 23 October 2026. Nothing in the app has changed yet. The "Meet your board" step (J-05, days 6–7) opens in the Today window like every other step. Under its one-line description, it shows who is on the leader's chapter board, from ISACA's records (in the prototype: the team's directory files, with invented people only).

1. The step, for Watanabe Kenji (Sakura Bay Chapter)

Each board member: initials, name, board role, term dates, and their time zone with the time it is there now (handy for planning a first board call). The leader is marked "you"; the previous president is marked as such, since the notebook step points to her. No emails or other contact details, as the plan says. Under the list: a privacy line, a pointer to the next step, and Mark as done.

Why these details: Camille confirmed on 21 October that ISACA keeps each board member's name, role and term dates, and that a president may see the member roster. The plan's rule for this screen is "names, roles and time zones only, no emails". Term dates are added because ISACA keeps them and they tell a new president who is staying and who is leaving soon.

The time shown: the time it is now in each member's city, from their time zone. Where it differs from the leader's, a short line says by how much. (The day of term still follows the demo date; only the clock is real.)
