Succession notebook: index, entry pages and decision records

Mockup for Matthew, 22 October 2026. Not built yet. The notebook stops listing every entry in full: it becomes a short index, and each entry opens on its own page inside the panel. From an entry, the president can print the decision, save it as a PDF, or email it. Records are filled in from the notebook's own data by a fixed template, never written by the AI model. Sample: Sakura Bay Chapter, with Sato Yumiko (outgoing president) and Watanabe Kenji (new president, day 3). Everything is invented.

1. The notebook as an index

2. An entry on its own page

3. The printed decision record

What's new behind this (for the build): a topic on each entry (a short fixed list: Elections, Finances, Meetings, Compliance, Membership, Events, Other), the date an entry was shared, follow-ups linked to an entry, and a record number. The notebook already stores the rest (who, role, question, answer, sources, decision line, shared yes/no, the date and the exact time). Printing uses the browser's own print window, so "Save as PDF" works with no extra software. Times show in the leader's time zone from the directory.
