# Outside governance sources: 20 free documents for Isa

For Matthew · 22 October 2026 · Camille's answer to question 11 (21 October): for broader governance questions, drawing on established best practice from ASAE, BoardSource, BoardEffect and Association Forum is fine, even ideal. ISACA process and policy stay with ISACA's own documents.

## Read this first: permission

**All four organizations reserve their rights.** Free to read does not mean free to copy into Isa:

| Organization | Terms of use | What it says about copying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BoardSource | https://boardsource.org/terms-of-use/ | No copying or republishing "without the prior express written consent of BoardSource." No personal-use exception found. |
| BoardEffect (owned by Diligent) | https://www.diligent.com/legal/terms-of-use | No copying, reproducing or republishing without Diligent's prior written consent; printing for personal, non-commercial use only. |
| ASAE | No terms page found; every page says "© Copyright ASAE. All rights reserved." | No reuse licence of any kind. |
| Association Forum | https://www.associationforum.org/terms-conditions | Information may be used "for personal or other non-commercial, educational purposes"; any reproduction needs written permission (info@associationforum.org). |

Camille's go-ahead settles which sources ISACA accepts. It doesn't give us those organizations' copyright permission. Loading the full text into Isa's database is copying, so the clean route is a short permission email to each organization (contact points above; ASAE's is https://www.asaecenter.org/contact-asae). This is not legal advice; i.c.stars staff may have a view on how a student prototype is treated.

**How these were found:** four research agents searched each site and opened every page listed here; all loaded in full with no login, payment or email form. None of the sites shows "most read" or download counts, so "most used" can't be measured: picks are by relevance to a chapter president, except where a site labels something "Popular". Section headings came from an automated page summary, so check each page before loading it.

## Demo questions these could answer (candidates)

Two or three simple questions whose answers would come from these documents. They need checking against the real text once loaded, since Isa's search matches words in the passages:

1. **"What should we cover when we orient new board members?"** BoardSource #1 and BoardEffect #2. Camille's own example, so it shows ISACA exactly what she approved.
2. **"What should go in our board meeting minutes?"** BoardEffect #3. It sits nicely next to ISACA's guidebook (the Secretary documents decisions), so the answer could show an ISACA source and an outside source side by side.
3. **"What do we do if a board member has a conflict of interest?"** BoardSource #4. A clear, practical answer (the chair names it, the member steps aside, the minutes record it).

Each answer should label the outside source plainly, for example "Best practice from BoardSource (not ISACA policy)", so a leader never mistakes it for ISACA's rules.
