# Speaking cards: Matthew, Sprint 3 (Kenji's demo)

Working draft, November 17, 2026. One card per stop in the demo, in the order Matthew clicks through Isa as Watanabe Kenji on day 3. Each card has what to click and what to say. The words are Matthew's to change.

Status of each card: **settled** means Matthew has agreed the wording; **draft** means Claude's first wording, not yet reviewed aloud.

Team only until the presentation: these cards describe the onboarding demo.

## Card 2 · Today (draft)

*Click: **Today** on the left rail.*

> This is Today. Think of it as Kenji's guide through his first 90 days. Isa knows what day of his term it is, and shows him only the steps that matter right now, a few at a time. On day three he sees two. On day sixty he'll see different ones. Nobody hands him a ninety-day checklist on day one.

## Card 3 · His Chapter Experience contact (draft)

*Click: **Meet your Chapter Experience contact**.*

> His first step is a person, not a document. This is his contact on your Chapter Experience team, and Isa gives him three ways to say hello: book a welcome call, in his own time zone. Send an introduction. Or join a group welcome with other new presidents.

*Click: **Write message**. Let the draft show.*

> If he'd rather write, Isa starts the note for him, and he can change every word. Isa never sends anything on her own.

*Click: **Cancel**. Don't press Send: it completes the step.*

## Card 8 · The succession notebook (settled)

*Click: **Succession Notebook** on the left rail.*

> That note came from here: the succession notebook. Every chapter has one, and it passes from president to president. Decisions, contacts, what worked, what's unfinished. These are the chapter's own words, with no AI in them.
>
> And Isa helps fill it. From his second week, she'll ask him one small question: "Anything from this week your successor should know?" One line is enough. So the notebook grows a little at a time, all year, and nobody has to write it all in the last week.

## Card 11 · The menu (settled)

*Click: **‹ Notebook**, then **⋯**.*

> A few more things live here. He can print or save the whole notebook, as his own copy or a copy for the board. He can sort it oldest first, and read Sato's year from the start. And he can choose how often Isa asks: every week, every two weeks, or every four.

*Click: **Removed entries**.*

> And nothing in the notebook is ever erased. If a president removes an entry, it comes off the working list, but it stays on the record: what it said, who removed it, and when.

*Click: **Done**.*

*Don't click "How the notebook works": it replaces the notebook with the guide.*

*Click: **Listen Library** on the left rail.*
