Terms Isa's documents use without defining

Second Star Collective · Isa team · November 4, 2026

Isa explains terms only in ISACA's own words. While checking her documents, we found 21 terms that the documents use but don't define, so she can't explain them yet, and one term chapter leaders need that the documents don't use (quorum). Would ISACA be willing to provide or approve a short definition for any of them?

  • Each row shows where the term appears and why a definition would help a chapter leader.
  • If there are other terms new leaders often ask you about, we'd be glad to add them.
  • Beyond the terms ISACA's documents already use, we left out tax and legal terms on purpose: their meaning changes from country to country, and Isa passes those questions to a person.

Where. "Guidebook" is the Chapter Leader Guidebook: President (August 2026). "Agreement" is the Chapter Affiliation Agreement (2024 template). Other names are the documents' own titles.
