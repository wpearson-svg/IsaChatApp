# Terms ISACA's documents use but don't define

November 3, 2026 · Matthew Brady, with Claude · Sprint 3

**What this is.** Isa's glossary shows only ISACA's own words: 26 terms that ISACA's documents define, each quoted word for word with its source (onboarding/glossary.json, checked by onboarding/check_glossary.py). While building it, we listed the terms the same documents use without defining. This page is that list, so ISACA can decide whether to provide or approve short definitions.

**Rules for this list.**

- A term here stays out of Isa until ISACA approves wording for it. Isa never writes her own definitions.
- Where a term appears is listed from ISACA's documents only, so this page can be shared with ISACA as it is. It doesn't mention the onboarding feature, which stays a surprise for the presentation.
- Not on the list, on purpose:
  - Tax terms. Their meaning changes from country to country, ISACA's documents don't cover them, and Isa's rules send tax questions to a person.
  - The Chapter Success Manager (Camille's caution, October 21).
- When ISACA sends definitions, add them to onboarding/glossary.json with ISACA as the source. Whether Isa also answers from them in the chat is a question for Willis, because it touches the search.

**Where.** "Guidebook" is the Chapter Leader Guidebook: President (August 2026). "Agreement" is the Chapter Affiliation Agreement (2024 template).

## Governance

| Term | Where it appears | Why a definition would help |
|---|---|---|
| AGM (Annual General Meeting) | Guidebook pp. 6, 8, 13, 14; Compliance Framework (AGM minutes) | The documents expand the letters but don't say what the AGM is for, or what must happen at it. Several deadlines are counted from it. |
| Bylaws | Guidebook pp. 2–4, 6–8, 11; Compliance Framework; Common Tasks; Agreement | The Agreement's definition is circular ("the Chapter's bylaws"). A sentence on what bylaws cover, and how they relate to ISACA's own rules, would help. |
| Minutes | Compliance Framework; Core Roles (Secretary); Common Tasks; Agreement | Required evidence for compliance, but what they must record isn't said. |
| Ex officio | Guidebook pp. 2, 3 | Presidents are "ex-officio members" of the SAC and of committees. Plain words would help readers whose first language isn't English. |
| Nominating Committee | Guidebook p. 3 | Named, but its role isn't described. |
| Fiduciary oversight | Guidebook pp. 5, 6 | A legal-sounding term that new leaders may not know. |
| Legal entity; good standing | Guidebook p. 3; Compliance Framework; Agreement | Required for compliance, but neither is explained. |
